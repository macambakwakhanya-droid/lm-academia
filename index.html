LM MATHEMATICS // MATERIAL
UPLOAD
Add papers, notes, and tutorials — sorted by category as you go.
Files upload to storage and sync across devices. They are opened through direct links, so if your storage bucket is public, anyone who has a file's link can open it. Only PDFs with selectable text are indexed (first 100 pages, in your browser). Scanned PDFs, images, videos and other formats are stored but are not searchable. Indexed text is used by Similar Questions and the AI Lab. It is not used by the workspace. Each file below shows its real indexing status. LM MATHEMATICS // PAST PAPERS
SIMILAR QUESTIONS
Find past-paper questions that resemble the one in front of you.
Matching uses a sentence-embedding model (MiniLM) running in your browser. Once at least 3 text chunks from your PDFs are indexed, search runs over your uploads, and results are excerpts of your files, not necessarily whole questions. Until then it searches a small built-in SAMPLE set. Those results are labelled SAMPLE and are not from your papers. The percentage is a similarity score, not a probability of being the same question.

LM MATHEMATICS // AI-ASSISTED
AI LAB
Ask a question about your module and get an AI-written answer.
Answers are written by an AI model and can contain mistakes. When relevant excerpts from your indexed uploads are found they are sent along, and the answer says which ones. Otherwise it says the answer comes from general knowledge. The model may run code for calculations when the backend allows it. If the final result is a derivative, integral, single-equation solution or number, this page then re-checks it itself with math.js and shows VERIFIED, MISMATCH or NOT CHECKED. That check is numeric and covers only the final result, not your explanation or a proof. Always check against your memos.
