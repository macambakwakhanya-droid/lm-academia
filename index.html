ABOUT LM
LM is a software engineering company — an agency building systems and models for focused, practical use rather than broad consumer products.
LM Mathematics is one such system: a personal tool built around a single module, designed to work closely with what a student actually uploads and does, rather than general-purpose tracking.
LM is founded and operated by Leo Macamba, and also runs Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Physics, Programming, Academic Writing, and Economics as separate divisions, alongside client services work.
For more on LM outside of this system, see LM Official.

TERMS & SERVICES
Last updated: 2026
LM Mathematics is provided as-is, as a personal support tool for your coursework in this module. It does not guarantee any grade, outcome, or result — what you get out of it depends on the work you put in alongside it.
This instance is scoped to one student and one module. Content you upload is used only to run the analysis and features within this system, and isn't shared outside it beyond what's needed to operate the system itself.
LM reserves the right to update these terms, or the system itself, at any time. Continued use after a change means you accept the current version.
Questions about these terms can be directed to Leo directly.

PRIVACY
Last updated: 2026
The name, degree, institution, and module you provide during setup are used only to tailor this instance to you, and are stored to your account rather than shared with any third party.
Your password is used only to lock this system to your account — it isn't used anywhere outside this system.
Papers and material you upload are used only to run this system's own analysis for you, not for any purpose beyond this system.
You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information at any time by contacting Leo directly.
